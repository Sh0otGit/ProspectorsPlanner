Course Planner

Turn your degree evaluation into a Spring 2027 schedule and a ready CRN list.

How it works

  1. Upload your degree evaluation.
  2. Pick the courses you still need.
  3. Mark hours you cannot attend. Optional.
  4. Compare instructors and add sections.
  5. Copy your CRNs into Goldmine.
Ratings weight UTEP course evaluations published under Texas HB 2504 and Rate My Professors equally. Sections come from the public Goldmine schedule.
