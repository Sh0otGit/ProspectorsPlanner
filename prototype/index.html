Know Your Schedule
Before You Touch Goldmine.

Easily compare top-rated professors from Rate My Professors and UTEP course evaluations, build a schedule with your full list of CRNs and avoid the clunky mess that is Goldmine.

Four Steps

  1. Complete your advising appointment.
  2. Search for your classes.
  3. Compare instructors and add sections.
  4. Copy your CRNs into Goldmine.
Instructor names, ratings and sections are real. Ratings blend UTEP's public HB 2504 evaluations with Rate My Professors, and sections come from the public Banner class schedule. Course selection is a real search against this term's offerings, not a personalized requirement list. See Methodology.
