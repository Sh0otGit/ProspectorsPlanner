Scoring
50% UTEP course evaluations, 50% Rate My Professors when both exist; 100% whichever one does when only one does. Evaluation averages are adjusted toward the university mean of 4.20 using a prior of 12 responses, so a 5.00 from four students ranks below a 4.80 from sixty. Rate My Professors reviews are a third-party, self-selected sample — not a UTEP source. Seat counts aren't shown: UTEP doesn't publish them anywhere public.
