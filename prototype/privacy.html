Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, nothing sold or shared. Long version below.

Last updated August 20, 2026.

What is stored, and where

Your in-progress picks (which courses you selected, which hours you blocked, which sections you added) are kept in your browser's sessionStorage so you can move between pages without losing your place. This clears automatically when you close the tab and never leaves your device: it is not sent to any server.

The admin panel's login session is a single HttpOnly cookie that exists only for the site operator, not for students using the planner. It carries no personal data, just a random session token.

What we collect

Course, section, and instructor data comes from public sources: UTEP's Banner class schedule, the HB 2504 instructor evaluation site, and Rate My Professors, all scraped and cached on our own server so the site does not have to hit those systems on every visit. None of these sources involve your personal data. They are public listings, not tied to you.

If you submit the anonymous "Rate this tool" feedback or use "Report a problem," we store the rating, the text you write, and, only if you choose to include one, a name or contact email. Nothing else about you is collected alongside it: no IP address logging tied to your submission, no account, no analytics tracking your visit.

Who this data is shared with

Nobody. Course lookups are not sold, shared, or used for advertising. There is no ad network and no third-party tracking script on this site.
