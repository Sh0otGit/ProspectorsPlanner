Terms of use

Plain-language terms for using an independent, unofficial planning tool.

Last updated August 18, 2026.

What this is

Prospector's Planner is an independent student project, not a UTEP service. It helps you review requirements, compare instructors, and build a schedule. It is a planning aid, not a system of record — it does not register you for classes, and it is not connected to Goldmine's registration system.

No guarantee of accuracy

Course listings, meeting times, instructors, and CRNs are scraped from UTEP's public schedule and refreshed on a regular but not real-time cadence (see Methodology). Seats, waitlists, and last-minute room or instructor changes are not available from any public source and are not shown here. Always confirm details in Goldmine before registering, and treat this site's CRN list as a starting point, not a guarantee that a section is still open.

Instructor ratings

Ratings blend UTEP's own HB 2504 evaluation data with Rate My Professors, both aggregated and adjusted as described in Methodology. They are opinions and statistics about past terms, not a promise about a future one.

Your account and submissions

There are no user accounts. If you submit feedback or a problem report, don't include anything you wouldn't want an operator reading — these are stored, not anonymized end-to-end, and aren't intended for anything but improving the tool.

Acceptable use

Don't use automated tools to scrape or overload this site, don't submit false or abusive content through the feedback or report forms, and don't attempt to access the admin panel without authorization.

No warranty

This tool is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. The operator is not liable for registration outcomes, missed deadlines, or decisions made based on data shown here.

Changes

These terms may change as the project develops. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.
