Rate My Professors — every 30 days
RMP's own Terms of Service prohibit automated access. Scraped anyway, a deliberate decision — see CLAUDE.md's Data sources entry and scrapers/rmp.js for the full reasoning. Two steps: the full campus professor list (~2,400 people, aggregate numbers only, ~24 requests), then a bounded review pull — only for instructors who also match someone actually teaching a real section this term, and only the handful of reviews RMP's own professor page shows, never a deeper pull.
